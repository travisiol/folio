## 1 — What it is

One token. Your whole portfolio.

Pick Robinhood Stock Tokens — NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, META… — set the weights, mint the basket as one token.
Redeem it any time and get the stocks back.

No custodian. No admin. The vault is the contract.
[link]

## 2 — What you gain

Why mint a basket on FOLIO:

→ One ERC-20 holds your whole portfolio. Send it, hold it, redeem it.
→ 1% of the basket's tokens = 1% of the stocks in its vault, always.
→ Create a basket others want and earn a fee (up to 2%) on every mint.

Real stock tokens, on chain, in one line of your wallet.
[link]

## 3 — How it works

Three steps:

1. Build: choose 2 to 10 stock tokens and their weights. You see each price and the value per basket token.
2. Mint: deposit the stocks in those proportions. The basket token is yours.
3. Redeem whenever you want: burn the token, receive the stocks.

Weights are fixed in token units at creation; their % value moves with prices.
